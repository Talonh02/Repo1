About

Born and raised in Duncan, BC, I moved to Edmonton to pursue higher education and compete for the Golden Bears wrestling program. Now graduating with a BCom in Finance and a Philosophy minor, I've returned to BC seeking opportunities where analytical rigor meets real-world impact.

My interests lie at the intersection of finance, geopolitics, history, and philosophy—particularly their implications for understanding global developments amidst the AI revolution. An avid reader of the Financial Times, I closely follow every model release and industry development in artificial intelligence.
