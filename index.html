About

Talon Hird is a BCom Finance graduate (2025) with a Philosophy minor from the University of Alberta and former Golden Bears Wrestling athlete. Born and raised in Duncan, BC, he moved to Edmonton for higher education and to compete for the Golden Bears wrestling squad, and has now returned to BC seeking employment opportunities.

His interests lie at the intersection of finance, geopolitics, history, philosophy, and their implications for understanding global developments amidst the AI revolution. An avid reader of the Financial Times, he closely follows every model release and industry development in the extended sphere of artificial intelligence. This website was built entirely using Claude Code, Anthropic's CLI coding agent.

The academic section showcases samples of his work demonstrating financial acumen, writing ability, presentation skills, and data science projects coded in R. He welcomes any questions about his experience and qualifications.
